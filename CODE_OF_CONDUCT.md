# Code of Conduct

Short version: **be respectful and constructive**. No harassment, no discrimination, no personal attacks — in issues, discussions, pull requests, everywhere.

- Assume good intent. Critique ideas, not people.
- English and French are both welcome here. Code, logs and documentation stay in English; translations live in the dedicated files.
- In case of dispute, the maintainer has the final say.

Reports: jdanoffre@gmail.com (subject `[conduct]`). Reports are confidential.

Long version: [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
